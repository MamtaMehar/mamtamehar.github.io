01 / About

Research that informs decisions

My work sits at the intersection of development economics, agricultural innovation, climate change and public policy. I bring interdisciplinary research and evaluation experience to questions about how evidence can support better decisions for farmers, institutions and development partners.

I have worked with CGIAR research centres, including IRRI, ILRI, IFPRI, CIMMYT and WorldFish. My research has examined technology adoption, farmer behaviour, gender-responsive innovation, impact assessment and sustainability transitions.

My recent focus includes climate adaptation and mitigation, evidence synthesis, and policy-oriented research for resilient and low-emission agri-food systems.

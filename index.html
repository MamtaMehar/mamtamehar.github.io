About

My work sits at the intersection of development economics, agricultural research, climate change, gender and public policy. I have worked across CGIAR centres, including IRRI, ILRI, IFPRI, CIMMYT and WorldFish, as well as with international development organisations and evaluation partners.

My experience spans policy-oriented research, monitoring and evaluation, mixed-methods studies, systematic reviews and evidence synthesis. I am particularly interested in translating complex research findings into practical insights for policymakers, development programmes and donors.

Current areas of focus include climate adaptation and mitigation, gender-responsive agricultural innovation, sustainability, food systems and evidence-informed development decisions.
